# Dashboard monitor photo credits

The taxi's head-unit screen (`src/vehicle/HeadUnit*.js`) shows real photos: a cat album and the
scenery behind the karaoke lyrics. Every photo below is from **Wikimedia Commons** and is
dedicated to the public domain under **CC0 1.0** (checked on each file's page: licence
template / structured data "copyright license: CC0"). Credited anyway. No Unsplash, Pexels or
Pixabay imports; no people's faces, brands or logos.

Each file was downloaded as a 960 px Commons thumbnail, resized to 512 px wide and saved as WebP
(EXIF stripped) in `public/assets/textures/screens/`. Nothing else was changed; the frames, date
stamps, hearts and captions are drawn over them at runtime.

Everything else on the screen (the lyric lines, title cards, the pale face in the scare glitch,
the stickers and tape on the monitor) is drawn in code. The song lyrics are original.
